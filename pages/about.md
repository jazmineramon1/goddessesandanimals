## Project Description

This collection explores the theme of Goddesses and Animals, featuring art and images from across time and cultures that represent female deities connected to animals in different ways. Some examples include goddesses directly associated with specific animals, such as Bastet, the Egyptian goddess of cats, while others show female deities alongside their patron animals, such as Athena with owls. The collection includes both historical artwork from the periods in which these goddesses were worshipped and more modern Western depictions, illustrating how their representations have evolved over time.This theme is important because it highlights the diversity of religious belief systems and challenges the idea that religion has always been centered around male, monotheistic figures. The collection demonstrates how many cultures across thousands of years placed women and goddesses in positions of power, associating them with important domains such as war, medicine, wisdom, and celebration. It also encourages viewers to reflect on humanity’s historical relationship with nature, as animals and the natural world were often viewed as sacred and deeply connected to spiritual life.

This collection may be especially interesting to students, researchers, and anyone interested in mythology, history, religion, or art. Viewers can use it to learn about different cultures, time periods such as ancient Greece and ancient Egypt, and the values reflected in their religious practices. It also offers an opportunity for casual audiences to discover new deities or explore connections between their favorite animals and mythological figures.The contents of this collection include historical artwork, sculptures, paintings, and more modern/western interpretations of female deities and their animal associations. These materials were gathered from historical records, museum collections, and online archives. Some of the most helpful resources have been The Cleveland Museum of Art collection where we found a statue of Taweret the Hippo Goddess, and The British Museum collection where we found a Bastet and kittens statue.
